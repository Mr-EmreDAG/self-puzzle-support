❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn my own photos into puzzles?
From the main screen, tap "Upload Photo". Select any picture from your device's photo gallery or take a new photo. Choose your preferred piece count and start solving!
Can I play without an internet connection?
Yes! All standard categories and built-in puzzle libraries are completely accessible offline. An internet connection is only needed when loading rewarded hints or custom photo generation.
How do I turn sound or vibration off?
Tap the Settings (gear icon) on the main menu or during puzzle play. You can freely toggle sound effects, haptic vibrations, and grid overlays on or off.
Are my personal photos uploaded to any server?
No. Your privacy is paramount. Custom photos remain stored 100% locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

🔒 Privacy Policy Summary

Self Puzzle is designed with a strict "privacy-first" architecture:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, or location data.
  • Local Photo Processing: Custom photos chosen from your gallery remain strictly on your device. We never upload or share your photos.
  • Advertising: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency and privacy guidelines.
  • No Account Required: You can enjoy the full game without creating an account or signing in.
